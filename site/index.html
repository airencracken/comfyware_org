Our approach

A smaller sort
of internet.

Some of the best places online are small. A family album. A board for the Sunday regulars. Somewhere to share what you’re making with people who care.

Comfyware is software for those places. Run by the people using it, with room for your own customs and house rules.

Before you settle in.

Can I sign up for an account here?

This is the home of the projects. To use them, install an app on a server you manage, or join an installation run by someone you know. Each community has its own accounts and rules.

Do I need to run both apps?

Either app works on its own. Imvault keeps your images and clips; Witmoot keeps your conversations. Witmoot also supports uploading images through Imvault when you want to use them together.

How can I get involved?

Try an app, tell us what worked, report a bug, or help with code and documentation. Each project page links to its repository. If you’d like to support development financially, there’s Ko-fi too.

Help keep the kettle on.

If these little tools make a place for your people, you can support their development on Ko-fi. Thanks for spending some time here.
