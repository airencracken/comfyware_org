Songstead 0.2.1

A quiet place for your people.

Recommendations keep the friend, group, and words they arrived with. Several friends can recommend the same music without turning it into several listening assignments. It can sit for six months without anything being wrong.

Recent shows what people shared with everyone here, newest first. Private recommendations stay out of that view. Your shelf is a quiet place for music left for you, and your listening notes stay private.

Prepare a Witmoot discussion when you want one, or keep an existing discussion link. Every application works independently. There are no read receipts, streaks, deadlines, or unsolicited reminders.

Annotation visibility is your choice: immediate, spoiler-free, or hidden. Set a listening position manually when you like; no streaming account or playback tracking is required.

Licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The Gentoo overlay includes OpenRC/systemd services, logging, and a private data directory. You can also isolate the service with optional Bubblewrap support.

Extremely eventual consistency: because your friends have lives.

Listen through the original provider or a supported embed. Songstead keeps recommendations and conversations; it does not host or stream music.

Invite your people. Make it yours.

Owners configure the welcome message, house rules, mascot and favicon from the administration page. Before you share a song.

Who can see what I share?

Everyone here means people signed in to your Songstead installation. There is no anonymous feed. A private gift is visible to you and its recipient, or to current members of its group. Leaving or being removed from a group ends access to that group's recommendations and comments.

How do I invite friends or connect Witmoot?

Sign in as an owner and open Admin. Instance settings controls joining and the Witmoot address. Invitations creates a private link that you copy once and send to your friend. Accounts manages invitation permission, roles, suspension and one-hour recovery links. Members can change their own passwords from their account page.

Does Recent fill my shelf?

No. Recent lets you browse what people shared with everyone, newest first. Opening a recommendation changes nothing. Shared music becomes part of your shelf when you choose to organize it or comment on it; direct and group gifts already have a place there.

Can my friends see my listening notes?

Your listening choices, ratings, personal notes, and saved discussion links belong to you. Comments are conversation with the recommendation's audience. Preparing a Witmoot discussion carries music details and a source link; it leaves friend notes, group context, and private reactions behind. You choose where to post in Witmoot.
