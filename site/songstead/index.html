Songstead · Music recommendations · 0.1.0 in preparation

Keep the good
songs close.

Your friend sends a song. You’re busy, the chat moves on, and the link gets buried. Songstead gives those recommendations a home, ready when you have a moment.

A new Comfyware project. The first release is prepared for sharing music with your community or sending it privately to a friend or group. Publication and installation links will appear here when the repository and release are available.

From someone who knows you.

A link for your people

Paste a music link and choose who can see it: everyone here, a friend privately, or a group privately. YouTube, Spotify, Bandcamp, and other links can all have a place on your shelf.

Listen in your own time

Browse by music, person, or group. Save, explore, listen, or dismiss in your own time. These choices are private; a recommendation is a gift, never an assignment.

A little conversation

A recommendation keeps its sender and their words. Leave a comment when something stays with you. Timestamps can mark a moment in a track; spoiler-free annotations wait until you choose to reveal them.

A place of your own

Go, SQLite, and HTMX keep hosting simple. Private accounts, backups, and account exports keep your music history yours. No ranked feed or pressure to keep checking in.

0.1.0 release preparation

A quiet place for your people.

Recommendations keep the friend, group, and words they arrived with. Several friends can recommend the same music without turning it into several listening assignments. It can sit for six months without anything being wrong.

Recent shows what people shared with everyone here, newest first. Private recommendations stay out of that view. Your shelf is a quiet place for music left for you, and your listening notes stay private.

Prepare a Witmoot discussion when you want one, or keep an existing discussion link. Every application works independently. There are no read receipts, streaks, deadlines, or unsolicited reminders.

Annotation visibility is your choice: immediate, spoiler-free, or hidden. Set a listening position manually when you like; no streaming account or playback tracking is required.

AGPL-3.0-or-later, with a master default branch. The Gentoo overlay and OpenRC/systemd packaging are being prepared alongside the first release.

Extremely eventual consistency: because your friends have lives.

Listen through the original provider or a supported embed. Songstead keeps recommendations and conversations; it does not host or stream music.

The Comfyware principles guide the project: comfortable software for a small community, run by the people using it.
