From someone who knows you.

A link for your people

Share a music URL with everyone here, a friend privately, or a private group. Listen through the original provider or a supported embed; Songstead does not host or stream music.

Every recommendation has a person

Matching URLs share a song card, keeping who recommended it, when, and what they said. Different providers stay separate.

Your own listening shelf

Save songs, like them privately, and leave listening notes for yourself. No public like counts or listening receipts. Posting a recommendation marks it as listened for you.

A little conversation

Comment with the recommendation’s audience, or choose to take a discussion to Witmoot. Your personal notes and likes stay private.

Extremely eventual consistency:
because your friends have lives.

No streaks, deadlines, unsolicited reminders, or playback tracking. Opening a recommendation changes nothing. Your shelf grows when you choose to take part.

Invite your people and set your own house rules. Songstead works on its own; every connection to another app is a choice. The Comfyware principles guide what belongs here.

A copy worth keeping.

The running service makes verified backups automatically each day. Keep a protected copy on another machine too, and practice restoring it. The backup and recovery guides explain what to keep, retention limits, and how to recover.

Make it yours

A shelf for your people.

Run a small instance, invite your friends, and put a reverse proxy in front. Songstead works independently; connect Witmoot when you want a place for longer discussions.

  1. Choose your installation.

    Use the Linux binary and source guide, or the Gentoo overlay.

  2. Set up your place.

    The deployment guide covers private storage, owner provisioning, backups, and native services. The administration guides cover invitations and connecting Witmoot.

  3. Leave a good song.

    Sign in, paste a provider link, choose everyone here or a private audience, and add a note if you like. Listen when you have a moment.

Before you share a song.

Who can see what I share?

Everyone here means people signed in to your Songstead installation. There is no anonymous feed. A private gift is visible to you and its recipient, or to current members of its group. Owner status does not bypass these boundaries.

Can my friends see my listening notes?

Your listening choices, likes, ratings, and personal notes belong to you. Comments are conversation with the recommendation’s audience. You choose whether to post a discussion in Witmoot.
