Somewhere to catch up.

Boards and archives

Organize topics into boards, reply in order, and search for things later. Archive older boards when you want to keep the memories.

Membership and permissions

Keep a personal notebook, invite a private group, or open the door more widely. Groups and individual permissions let you decide who can read and post.

At your own pace

Chronological conversations and ordinary pages. No ranked feed, infinite scroll, or streak to keep alive.

Hosting and backups

One Go binary and one data directory. Set your own house rules, manage your boards, and back up your installation.

Make it yours

Start a little gathering.

Witmoot runs on your own server. It works independently, and you can connect Imvault when you want somewhere to keep shared images.

  1. Pick your installation.

    Follow the binary and Debian package guide, or use the Gentoo overlay. The README also covers the local demo and building from source.

  2. Set the table.

    Use the deployment guide to create your owner account, set up HTTPS, and run the service.

  3. Make room for your people.

    Create a few boards, choose who can join, and send out invitations. The first conversation can be as simple as saying hello.
